# Car Battle Lobby spec (carter's 70 card picks, 2026-10-20)

Reference sheet: "CAR BATTLE LOBBY" (chunky Roblox style, 220 x 180 studs, max height 32).
Colors: asphalt #485363, orange #F58220, blue #258DDA, tire #24272B, grass #7FBA42, markings #EDF0EA.
The old warehouse lobby is archived in `archive/lobby_v1/` (shipped to ServerStorage.LobbyArchive, never run). Nothing of it is kept.

## Layout
- Same size as the picture. Spawn on the front path facing the center.
- Players walk, faster than normal run speed, cannot climb props, see garages from the front only (no walking inside), garage doors always open.
- Light gray paths, asphalt ground like the duel arena, tire walls with orange lights at the edge, steady orange lights like the picture (not more).
- Same trees as the arena (round + pine, rocks, bushes, flowers), orange/blue X flags at the entrances, benches, cones, tool carts, big CAR VS CAR sign on an entrance arch over the spawn path.
- Sunny, puffy clouds, soft shadows, hills far away, dirt paths outside. No light towers. Duel arena NOT visible. No live-fight screen.
- Lobby music spot, silent until carter gives an id.

## PLAY
- 4 small mini PLAY pads (orange/blue rings, glow + spin) in a ring at the center where the big pad is.
- Step on a pad OR tap the PLAY screen button (it walks you onto a pad). 2 players per pad; friends challenge by standing on the same pad.
- Waiting: "Finding player..." on screen, "1/2" on the pad. Nobody joins = wait forever (no bot fill). Leave by walking off.
- Before a match: car pick screen. Fade into the fight. Coming back, land next to the pads.

## Center workshop
- Keeps the big wrench sign. Holds a GIANT gumball machine full of car capsules.
- Odds on screen before every pull AND on a sign by the machine. Big pull animation (shake, capsule pops, opens).
- Rare pulls announced in chat to everyone. Also shows the rarest gumball car on display.

## Customize garage
Pick car + paint, car on a spinning turntable, opens with a press-E / tap prompt.

## Shop
Sells coin packs + VIP pass (ids from carter only, never make up) + paints/horns/trails. The 3 cars inside show off rare gumball cars (Ram Truck, Cannon Van, Saw Buggy). Opens with a press-E prompt at the counter.

## Boards
- TOP DRIVERS = weekly wins (resets Monday). WINS = all-time wins. Top 5 with avatar heads. Physical boards only.
- #1 player gets a gold car on the workshop stage and a crown tag.

## Other
- Each player's car parked in a spot with their name.
- Screen buttons: just PLAY, CARS, SHOP. Coins top left, level/XP bar under them. Daily gift as a screen button.
- Name tags show name + level. No click-a-player stats.
- Glowing arrow points new players to the pads.
- If phones lag, cut far trees first.
- UI art is carter's: placeholder UI only.
