# How to beat each weapon (for the tips and the VS screen)

Every weapon waits 6 seconds. Hits only count at the right moment, so the plan is the same for all: make the first move miss, then punish while it waits.

## Metal Ram (Ram Truck, heavy)
- It charges in a straight line (the stick bends it a little, about 30%, and auto-aim helps a bit).
- Counter: wait until the charge starts, then turn sharply away at the last moment. A missed charge makes the truck slide (0.45 s) and it cannot charge again for 6 s.
- Then drive in and crash into it while it recovers. A faster car wins a crash.

## Roof Cannon (Cannon Van, middle)
- One slow shell, 26 damage, curves a little toward you (auto-aim) and bounces off one wall.
- Counter: shells are slow and easy to follow with the eyes. Move sideways right after you see the shot (the orange line shows where it will go). Get close: the van is weak up close.
- During its 6 s wait it cannot shoot, so rush it then.

## Spinning Saw (Saw Buggy, fast and light)
- Hurts only while the front touches you and the player holds the button (3 s, then a 6 s wait). Damage builds the longer it stays on you.
- Counter: kite. Keep moving and keep your tail away from its nose. Break contact for a moment to reset the build-up.
- It has the least HP, so one good hit or crash hurts it a lot.

## Specials (the SPECIAL button fills by hitting)
- Shock (Ram): a ring that shoves and hurts anything close. Stay out of 4.5 car-lengths when it glows.
- Triple (Van): three shells fanned out. Do not dodge to the side, dodge back or forward.
- Saw throw (Buggy): a big blade that curves hard. Turn toward it and drive past it.
